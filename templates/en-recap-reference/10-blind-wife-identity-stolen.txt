the blind wife accidentally turns on the TV
she is shocked
hearing her husband say in an interview
that he is married
but the woman he shows to the public is not her
instead he lets another woman take her place
becoming the rightful rich wife in everyone's eyes
on screen they act close with no shame
and even announce she is pregnant with his child
Ana feels her heart break completely
she struggles trying to go ask him what is going on
but suddenly her body gives out
she falls hard onto the ground
her head hits the couch hard
and she passes out right there
when she wakes up again
her eyesight comes back like a miracle
she stands up in excitement
wanting to tell her husband right away
but outside the study
she overhears him talking with a friend
it turns out after she lost her sight in the accident
Benjamin's family always looked down on her
so he let that woman take over her identity
what is even worse to keep Ana trapped by his side
he installed signal blockers in the villa
cutting her off from the outside world
and for five years
what he gave her was not real medicine
just regular health supplements
he personally cut off her last chance to see again
hearing all this Anna feels completely broken inside
she decides she will not be controlled anymore
so she contacts her old dance mentor
planning to finish paperwork and return to the stage
but before that she wants to expose everything herself
since they love acting
she decides to keep pretending she cannot see
and watch how long they keep this up
sure enough the woman shows up
Benjamin lies saying she is a new nutritionist
and she flirts with him right in front of Anna
enjoying everything that should belong to Anna
even worse she quickly starts picking on her
knowing Ana is allergic to lilies
but still sends a huge bouquet
causing a serious allergic reaction
yet Benjamin does nothing to stop it
and when Ana throws the flowers away
he blames her for being selfish and inconsiderate
but the woman does not stop there
she brings in a dirty dog from a trash area
saying it will be a guide dog for Anna
but every word is full of insult
the next second the dog loses control and jumps at Anna
at first Benjamin stands in front to protect her
but when the woman pretends to be scared
he turns and runs to her without thinking
letting Ana get knocked down
and even bitten on the leg
at the hospital
Ana hears an even harsher truth from a nurse
there was only one surgeon that night
Benjamin made him treat the woman's minor injury first
ignoring Ana's critical condition
almost costing her life
at that moment her last bit of hope is gone
after recovering she finishes all paperwork
planning to leave the next day
but something else happens
Benjamin's enemy John comes for revenge
and kidnaps all three of them
facing this man who lost everything
Benjamin feels fear for the first time
but John says killing him alone is too easy
then he points at Ana and the other woman
and sets up a cruel game
one of them must stay in a freezing truck
for half an hour
life or death depends on luck
facing his terrified wife and the other woman
Benjamin chooses Ana without hesitation
at that moment she feels shocked and heartbroken
she cannot believe the man who loved her is this cold
when she is pulled out of the truck
her body is shaking from the cold
Benjamin still pretends to care and walks over
then John starts the second game
he asks who will be pushed into icy water
after looking at the weak acting woman
Benjamin still does not hesitate
he chooses Ana again who is already weak
and even promises to make it up to her later
watching her get pushed into the water
but it still does not end
John quickly brings up a third game
he asks him to leave one hostage behind
while he hesitates
the woman suddenly holds her stomach
crying saying she is pregnant
and puts on a fake miscarriage act
for the sake of the so called child
Benjamin abandons Ana again
while he leaves with the other woman
when his figure disappears Ana finally lets him go
she decides she does not need his care anymore
not even him as a person
after somehow escaping
Anna returns to the home that once felt warm
this home once was her safe place
now it feels like a prison for her dignity
in despair
Anna takes off the ring she wore for five years
saying in another life she will never love him again
then she sets the mansion on fire
and disappears from his world forever
