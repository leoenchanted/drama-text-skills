# English Style Polish Rules

Use these rules after learning the reference scripts and before final output.

The goal is not literary English.  
The goal is natural, dramatic, ready-to-record English recap voiceover.

## Core Style

- Keep lines short.
- Keep verbs active.
- Put emotional turns early.
- Prefer clear subject + action.
- Make every line easy for AI voiceover.
- Sound like a native English recap channel, not a translated Chinese script.

## Avoid Stiff Translated Phrases

Avoid overusing:

- after hearing this
- at this moment
- the woman felt very sad
- the man was very angry
- she was very disappointed
- upon hearing that
- to her surprise
- at this point
- in her heart
- the woman is laughing at her stupidity
- became extremely anxious
- the more she spoke the more agitated she became

Better alternatives:

- that was when she understood
- that was when her heart finally went cold
- she stayed silent
- he had no idea
- she already knew the truth
- the trap had already been set
- she did not cry
- she did not explain
- she only waited
- his face changed
- the room went silent
- the truth hit him too late

## Reduce Repetition

Do not repeat the same transition phrase too often.

Limit these phrases unless they are truly needed:

- but he did not know
- what he did not know was
- at that moment
- the next second
- little did he know
- even worse

Rotate with:

- he had no idea
- she had already seen through it
- that was only the first trap
- then came the real blow
- this time, she did not stop them
- she let them finish the lie
- the cruelest part was
- the answer was worse than she imagined
- by the time he realized it
- she was already gone

## Stronger Emotional Lines

Weak:

```text
she felt very sad
```

Stronger:

```text
that was the moment
her last hope in him died
```

Weak:

```text
he was very regretful
```

Stronger:

```text
only then did regret
start tearing him apart
```

Weak:

```text
the woman was angry
```

Stronger:

```text
she did not scream
she simply began planning his downfall
```

## Heroine Voice

The heroine should not sound helpless forever.

After the breaking point, make her:

- calm
- observant
- strategic
- emotionally detached
- ready to leave
- ready to expose the truth
- ready to take back control

Useful lines:

- she did not expose them yet
- she wanted to see how far they would go
- this time, she chose silence
- because silence was part of the trap
- she had already prepared the evidence
- she was no longer begging for love
- she was collecting proof
- she was done being their free servant

## Male Lead Or Villain Regret

Regret should arrive too late.

Useful lines:

- he finally understood
- but the woman he hurt
  had already stopped waiting
- he wanted to explain
- but she no longer needed the truth from him
- he thought she was bluffing
- until the divorce papers became real
- by the time he reached for her
- she had already walked away

## Villain Language

Make the villain's goal clear and hateable.

Good villain functions:

- wants the heroine's position
- wants the child
- wants the inheritance
- wants the house
- wants the company
- wants public recognition
- wants the male lead to abandon the heroine

Do not make the villain's scheme vague.

## Voiceover Line Breaks

Break lines by emotional beat, not grammar.

Good:

```text
She did not expose them
because she wanted to see
how far her best friend
was willing to go
```

Avoid:

```text
She did not expose them because she wanted to see how far her best friend was willing to go.
```

## Final Polish Pass

Before output:

1. Remove stiff translated phrases.
2. Shorten long lines.
3. Replace flat emotion words with dramatic action or consequence.
4. Remove repeated transitions.
5. Make the hook sharper.
6. Make the cliffhanger less complete.
